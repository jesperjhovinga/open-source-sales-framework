# Positioning — Example-Corp

## Propositions

### `example_prop_a` — FieldPilot Platform
**Narrative arc:** Mid-size field-service contractors run their operation on
spreadsheets, whiteboards and five disconnected point tools. Every dispatch
decision is a guess; every invoice lags the job by days. FieldPilot puts
scheduling, dispatch, job tracking and invoicing in one system built for
50–500-technician operations — not enterprise bloat, not small-shop toys.
**Key messages:** One system of record for the field. Dispatch decisions from
live data, not radio calls. Cash in the door days faster.
**Objection reframes:**
- "We already use <point tool>" → keep it; FieldPilot replaces the glue-work
  between tools, and that glue is where your margin leaks.
- "Our dispatchers won't adopt new software" → adoption is a design constraint
  we accept: dispatch board mirrors the whiteboard they already use.

### `example_prop_b` — Implementation Services
**Narrative arc:** Software fails in field service when rollout ignores the
crew. Our implementation team has done 80+ rollouts; we carry the change
management so the ops manager doesn't have to.
**Key messages:** Live in 6 weeks. Crew adoption as a deliverable, not a hope.

## Boundaries (what we DON'T do)
- No custom development per customer — configuration only.
- No hardware. We integrate with GPS/telematics vendors, we don't sell devices.
