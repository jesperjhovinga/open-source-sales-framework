---
name: grill-me
description: Stress-test a plan or strategy by being interviewed relentlessly until every branch of the decision tree is resolved. Use when the user says "grill me", wants to pressure-test a BD approach, outreach strategy, automation design, or any plan before committing to it.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for my response before continuing.

If a question can be answered by reading existing files or context in the conversation, use that instead of asking.
